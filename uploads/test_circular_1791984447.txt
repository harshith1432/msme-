--- Master Circular on CGTMSE Coverage 2026 ---
Ministry of MSME and SIDBI announce enhanced guarantee coverage up to Rs 5 Crore.

Section 1: Collateral-Free Lending Requirements
All scheduled commercial banks and NBFCs shall extend collateral-free loans to Micro and Small Enterprises with an annual guarantee fee concession of 10% for women and SC/ST entrepreneurs.

Section 2: Claim Settlement Timelines
Guaranteed claims must be formally processed and credited within 30 statutory days from default notification.
